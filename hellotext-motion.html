Every signal has a next move.

A self-directed concept piece for Hellotext — visualizing how one customer signal becomes a decision, a message, and a sale, entirely in motion.

Scroll to watch it unfold
01 — Signal

A customer goes quiet.

Half a cart, a paused scroll, a question left mid-sentence — signals most stores never even see, let alone act on.

02 — Decide

The system decides.

Not a rule, not a generic template — a single decision, made from what this customer actually did, not what most customers do.

03 — Act

It reaches out.

A message sent on the channel they already have open — no app to download, no form standing between the question and the answer.

Still deciding on the jacket? Free returns if the fit's not right.
04 — Close

The loop closes.

The conversation ends in a sale, and the system remembers what worked — so the next signal gets a sharper decision.
